Architectural Masterpiece

Perched high above the iconic Beverly Hills skyline, The Horizon Glass Villa represents the pinnacle of contemporary modernist architecture. Designed by award-winning Vanguard Studios, this exceptional residence seamlessly blurs the boundary between indoor luxury and dramatic natural topography. Featuring expansive floor-to-ceiling motorized glass panels, cantilevered living spaces, and exquisite organic finishes, every square foot is calibrated for effortless entertaining and meditative privacy.

The open-concept main floor flows seamlessly onto a suspended cantilevered travertine deck featuring an infinity-edge pool that mirrors the Pacific horizon. Smart home automation controls climate, lighting, security, and audio-visual systems autonomously throughout the estate.
